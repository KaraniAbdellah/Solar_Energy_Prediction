Project Overview

The Solar Energy Predictor provides real-time renewable energy yield estimates for solar installations by leveraging deep learning techniques. By analyzing meteorological factors such as Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI), thermal coefficients, relative humidity, and daylight duration ratios, the neural network models non-linear power yield patterns accurately.

Accurate Forecasting

Minimizes forecasting error margin via log-transformed feature inputs and neural non-linear activations.

Grid Optimization

Assists micro-grids and battery storage systems with energy storage planning and peak-load allocation.

Scalable Inference

Containerized inference service running on Google Cloud Run for rapid and consistent response times.
